## Closed issues and decisions

- [Ziyao Wang](ziyao.wang@se19.qmul.ac.uk) managed to compile and run the template ONOS App in ONOS SDN controller. He then added packet-capture functionality to the APP so that all ethernet packets through the OVS were presented in ONOS controller.
- [Ziyao Wang](ziyao.wang@se19.qmul.ac.uk) found that vEOS is incompatible with ONOS in `openflow`, `netconf`, or `restconf` SB protocol and there was no prebuilt drivers for vEOS in ONOS (the restArista driver is not usable). So, he discarded this approach.
